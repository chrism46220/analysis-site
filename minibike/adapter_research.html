Mega Moto 80 Adapter Plate — Research Summary

Product Identity

  • Product: Mega Moto 80 Adapter Plate (196/212/225 Engines)
  • SKU: MMFAMPBENT
  • Vendor: GoPowerSports (https://www.gopowersports.com)

Pricing

Variant Price
Plate only (base) $29.95 (retail $43.95, $14.00 discount)
Plate + Hardware kit $43.95 (includes nuts, bolts, washers)

SKU for the bare plate appears to be MMFAMPBENT; the hardware-included variant is a product option/add-on.

Description (from vendor)

"This heavy-duty steel adapter plate allows you to bolt a Tillotson, Predator, or Honda Clone engine (196cc, 212cc, or 225cc) directly onto your Mega Moto 80, 105, or E1000 frame — no cutting or welding required!"

What's Included

  • 1× Heavy-Duty Steel Bent Adapter Plate
  • 8 sets of 5/16" mounting nuts and bolts (when ordered with hardware)

Key Features

  • Slotted mounting holes — allows engine to slide forward/backward for chain tension adjustment when using a centrifugal clutch.
  • Bolt-on installation — adapts the larger 196/212/225cc engine footprint to the stock 79cc Mega Moto frame mounting pattern.
  • CNC/Laser-cut steel — precision-machined, heat/paint-finished.

Compatibility

  • Frames: Mega Moto 80, Mega Moto 105, Mega Moto E1000
  • Engines (direct swap):
  • Predator 212cc (and other Harbor Freight 79/212 clones)
  • Honda GX200 / Clone 196cc
  • Tillotson 196cc / 212cc / 225cc
  • Stock engine replaced: Mega Moto 79cc

Builder's Tech Note: Clearance Modifications Required

The vendor documents that the following modifications are needed for a 212cc engine swap, even with the adapter plate:

  1. Fuel tank must be relocated — the factory top-mounted gas tank will hit the top frame tube. You'll need a replacement tank mounted elsewhere.
  2. Stock air box will not fit — a Stage 1 Performance Kit or low-profile pod filter is needed.
  3. Stock exhaust will not fit — the box muffler hits the frame. Recommended: Skinny Center Exit Header Pipe (196cc/212cc), or a "Build Your Own" header kit.
  4. Torque converter clearance — if using a torque converter instead of a centrifugal clutch, the adapter plate raises the engine for clearance (per reviews: "Got the 212 back on level and up for torque converter clearance too!").

Dimensions / Hole Spacing

The product page does not publish exact hole spacing or plate dimensions. The plate has: - Pre-drilled holes matching the stock Mega Moto 79cc frame mount pattern - Slotted mounting holes on the engine side for fore-aft chain tension adjustment - 5/16" bolt size for included hardware

Summary

The MMFAMPBENT is a $29.95 heavy-duty bent steel adapter plate that enables bolting a 196–225cc engine onto a Mega Moto 80/105/E1000 frame without welding. It uses slotted holes for chain tension adjustment. Despite being "bolt-on," builders should plan for fuel tank relocation, an aftermarket air filter, and a custom exhaust. The plate is well-reviewed (4.9★ average across related products) but lacks published dimensions on the product page. The upgraded GPSFAMP adjustable plate ($49.95) is a more universal alternative but not Mega Moto-specific.
