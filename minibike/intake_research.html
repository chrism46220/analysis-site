Notes & Caveats

  1. Mega Moto 80 OEM engine: The stock Mega Moto 80 typically ships with a 196cc engine, not a 212. If the bike has a stock 196cc, the SBK102 still works (it covers 196cc engines too).
  2. Jetting: Adding a pod filter will lean the air/fuel mixture. A jet upgrade is recommended. GoPowerSports sells jet kits.
  3. Hemi vs Non-Hemi: SBK102 fits both. No special adapter needed.
  4. Frame clearance unknown in detail: Forum searches (oldminibikes.com, diygokarts.com) were inaccessible during research. The combo is likely to clear based on the adapter's low profile and similar builds, but first-hand MM80 frame confirmation would be useful.
