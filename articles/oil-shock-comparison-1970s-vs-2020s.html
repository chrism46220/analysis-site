Bottom Line: The 2020s shock differs fundamentally because of the ongoing energy transition. While oil‑price spikes still hurt transportation and energy‑intensive sectors, the winners are now aligned with decarbonization (renewables, EVs, storage) rather than with fossil‑fuel production alone. Policy, technology, and capital flows have created a structural shift that makes the current environment more likely to accelerate the energy transition than to revert to a fossil‑fuel‑dominant equilibrium.
