## 8. Historical Analog

### This week most resembles: **COVID recovery (March-November 2020)** and **early 2023 AI rally** — but importantly different.

**Why 2020 COVID recovery is relevant:**
- A massive geopolitical overhang (war/conflict) is unexpectedly resolved, triggering a relief rally
- Oil prices collapse (supportive for consumers, disinflationary)
- VIX collapses from 20s to mid-teens
- Broad-based rally across sectors, not just tech

**Why it's different:**
- 2020 was a liquidity crisis followed by ZIRP/QE infinity. Today we have 4.4% rates.
- 2020 saw massive fiscal stimulus. Today, fiscal deficit is a constraint.
- 2020 valuations were lower. Today's are elevated.

**Why early 2023 AI rally is similar:**
- Generative AI catalyst driving tech narrative
- NVDA leading, then rotation broadening out
- Valuations treated as irrelevant during "paradigm shift" phase

**Why NOT 2008 or dot-com:**
- Credit markets (HYG $80.03) are healthy, not stressed
- VIX is 16, not 40+
- No housing or systemic financial risk
- Dot-com 2000: earnings didn't support valuations. AI infra earnings ARE real (NVDA $130B+ revenue).

**Conclusion:** This rally has more in common with 2020 relief rallies (geopolitical catalyst) than with 2008 or dot-com. The key risk is valuation — not crisis.
